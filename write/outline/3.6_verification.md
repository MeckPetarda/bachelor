### End-to-end processing time

- The pipeline has a deterministic minimum delay by design: 5 s scan window (IR trigger to scan batch publish) +
  `activityTimeoutMs` (4 s default, cluster considered closed) + sweeper polling interval (2 s) + Navigo3 HTTP
  round-trip; theoretical minimum ~ 11 s under ideal conditions
- Measured as wall-clock time from IR trigger to Navigo3 attendance record appearing; measured in seconds
- Goal: confirm measured time is consistent with the theoretical minimum and does not exhibit unexplained outliers
