# Development Log - KiCad Schematic Work & Initial Circuits

**Date:** February 1–2, 2026
**Task:** Begin hardware schematic design for production PCB

## Objective

Transition from breadboard prototype to a designed PCB. First step is capturing the existing dev board wiring as a schematic in KiCad, establishing the baseline from which the production design will be derived.

---

## 1. Schematic Capture

Started a faithful schematic recreation of the current development board wiring. The goal at this stage is accuratly capturing what is physically wired, not yet what should be wired for production. This serves as the documented baseline and diff target for subsequent design decisions.

Components captured:

- ESP32-WROOM-32 with all active GPIO assignments
- YPD-R300 UHF RFID reader module with UART connections
- Status LEDs (WiFi, MQTT, scanning) with current-limiting resistors
- Button inputs (BUTTON1, BUTTON2) with pull-up resistors
- Power rail connections
