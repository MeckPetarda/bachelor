# Development Log - Second Lighthouse Dev Board Assembly

**Date:** February 19–20, 2026
**Task:** Assemble a second functional lighthouse dev unit

## Objective

Bring a second lighthouse online to enable development of group-based direction detection. The server-side traversal logic requires two lighthouses reporting to the same group — without a second physical unit, this cannot be developed or tested end-to-end.

---

## 1. Hardware Assembly

### Configuration

The second unit is assembled from available components and is intentionally a non-production dev board. It differs from the primary unit in one significant way: the battery charging circuit (TP4056 + DW01HA) is absent. The unit is powered directly via USB through the ESP32 development board's onboard regulator. Due to the lack of currently available hardware I repurpoused the esp previously used as the makeshift AP. I bought a new router to use as an AP.

| Aspect               | Primary Unit             | Secondary Unit                   |
|----------------------|--------------------------|----------------------------------|
| ESP32 form factor    | ESP32-WROOM-32 dev board | Different ESP32 dev board format |
| Power                | Full charger circuit     | USB via ESP dev board            |
| RFID reader          | YPD-R300                 | YPD-R300                         |
| Firmware             | Identical                | Identical                        |

This is sufficient for development purposes — the charger circuit is not relevant to scan delivery or MQTT communication.

---

## 2. What This Unblocks

With two lighthouses operational, the following server-side development becomes possible:

- Pairing lighthouses into groups (INSIDE + OUTSIDE placement)
- Processing paired raw scan streams into compound traversal events
- Determining entry/exit direction from the temporal relationship between the two scan timestamps

The lighthouses themselves remain concerned only with raw tag detections. All direction inference logic lives on the server.

---

**Status:** Two lighthouse dev units operational. Ready to proceed with server-side group processing and direction detection development.
